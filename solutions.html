How I Build Analytics Solutions

Transforming raw business data into meaningful insights using Microsoft Fabric, Power BI and SQL.

My delivery process

  1. 1

    Understand the business question

    Agree the decisions the analytics must support, who will use it and how success is measured.

  2. 2

    Assess and ingest the data

    Profile the sources, then bring data in with Fabric pipelines or Dataflows Gen2.

  3. 3

    Land and refine in OneLake

    Store a single copy of data and refine it through bronze, silver and gold layers.

  4. 4

    Model for analysis

    Shape curated data into facts and dimensions in a Lakehouse or Warehouse using SQL.

  5. 5

    Build the semantic model

    Define relationships, DAX measures and row-level security once, for every report.

  6. 6

    Design and deliver reports

    Build Power BI reports around the questions agreed at the start, then validate with users.

  7. 7

    Govern, support and improve

    Monitor refreshes, document the solution and iterate as the business changes.
