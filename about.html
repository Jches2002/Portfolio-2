The person behind the reports

About Me

I combine application support experience, logistics knowledge and a growing specialism in analytics.

I am an Application Support Analyst - BI working in the freight forwarding and logistics industry. My role sits between business systems, data and the people who use them, which gives me a practical view of what reporting needs to achieve.

How I moved into BI

I started through a Level 3 Digital Support Technician apprenticeship before moving into application support. I later completed a Level 4 Data Analyst apprenticeship with Distinction, turning a long-standing interest in data and technology into a specialist area.

What I work with

My work covers Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, SQL and DAX, alongside my knowledge of CargoWise and freight forwarding processes. I hold the Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate certification and the CargoWise Certified Professional certification.

What matters to me

I enjoy turning operational data into clear reporting that people can actually use. My support background keeps me focused on reliability, maintainability and the real business question rather than making something complicated for the sake of it.

Outside of work

Outside of BI, I enjoy running, going to the gym, hiking and playing padel. I have completed half marathons and I am working towards a marathon next year.
