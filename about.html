About

Business intelligence with an engineering mindset.

I am an Application Support Analyst - BI in the freight forwarding and logistics industry. My day-to-day work sits where systems, data and the people who rely on them meet, which gives me a practical view of what reporting needs to do for an operational business.

Journey into BI

I started in digital support through the Level 3 Digital Support Technician apprenticeship, then moved into application support. The Level 4 Data Analyst apprenticeship, which I completed with a Distinction, turned an interest in data into a specialism.

Fabric and Power BI

I hold the Microsoft Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate certification (DP-600). I work across Fabric, Power BI, SQL and DAX, and I care as much about the data layers underneath a report as the report itself.

Analytics engineering focus

My aim is to build analytics that are reliable, well modelled and easy to maintain. That means clear data preparation, sound semantic models and governance that holds up as more people use the data. My support background keeps me focused on solutions that work in production, not only in demos.
